## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the serene, grounded essence of Indonesian mountain agro-tourism. It balances warm rural hospitality (*keramahan desa*) with contemporary, high-performing clarity. The aesthetic draws heavily from Organic Modernism: deep verdant foliage, rich volcanic terracotta, and sun-bleached sandstone textures paired with generous breathing room and human-centered typography.

Targeted at domestic families, urban escape seekers, nature retreats, and eco-conscious travelers, the UI evokes feelings of calm, fresh mountain air, reliability, and warm communal welcome. High-fidelity visual cards frame lush agricultural landscapes, while crisp typography and intentional CTAs (specifically direct conversational pathways) keep the experience frictionless, grounded, and refreshingly devoid of bureaucratic clutter.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural geography of highland agro-tourism:
- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Deep Forest Green serves as the anchor for primary buttons, structural headlines, footers, and solid brand elements. It conveys maturity, shade, and permanence.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F`)**: Fresh Leaf Green operates as supporting UI tones, active indicators, and secondary badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97736`)**: Terracotta Clay is an energetic warm accent representing fertile soil, pottery, and sunset ridgelines. Use it judiciously for promotional highlights, package discounts, and seasonal tags.
- **Neutral (`#F4F1EA`) & Surface Cream (`#FAF9F6`)**: Replaces sterile stark white with warm limestone and sand undertones, reducing eye strain and evoking organic paper.
- **WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`)**: Reserved strictly for high-conversion conversational booking triggers, ensuring zero friction across customer touchpoints.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to reflect modern Indonesian digital design—friendly, readable, yet highly structured. 

- **Display & Headlines**: Emphasize natural rhythm using tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ground outdoor adventure and homestay titles with authority and presence.
- **Body Content**: Tuned for maximum legibility against warm natural surfaces with a generous 1.5x to 1.55x line-height ratio, facilitating long-form reading on village history, activities, and itinerary packages.
- **Labels & Badges**: Feature slight positive tracking for crisp glanceability on metadata like pricing, duration, and difficulty level.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a fluid responsive grid built on an 8pt spatial baseline:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid, max-width container of 1240px, with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4-column layout prioritizing vertical linear flows, utilizing `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins to guarantee thumb-friendly reachability.

Layouts should favor asymmetric content blocks for nature highlights, punctuated by full-bleed photography sections and warm container panels with consistent internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through soft, organic tonal layering and warm ambient diffusion rather than harsh technical drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: Sand neutral canvas (`#F4F1EA`) with flat card containers styled in `#FAF9F6`.
- **Level 1 (Hover / Static Cards)**: 0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06). Soft forest-tinted shadow providing gentle tactile lift.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays, Sticky Mobile WhatsApp Bar)**: 0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12), ensuring clear separation without visual heaviness.
- **Subtle Edging**: Surfaces use hairline borders of `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)` to maintain shape definition across differing screen brightness levels.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary, matching the soft rolling hills and organic contours of rural agro-tourism. 

Standard components (buttons, text inputs, filter chips) feature `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii. Product cards, hero banners, and floating bottom sheets use `rounded-lg` (16px) and `rounded-xl` (24px) respectively, evoking warmth, safety, and an approachable character. Circular pill forms are reserved for tags, status indicators, and floating booking CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) background, Sand Neutral text, `0.5rem` radius, bold label. Hover shifts to `#2D6A4F`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Booking Action**: Distinctive `#25D366` surface with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` or dark forest text. Features an integrated WhatsApp glyph, pulsing micro-interaction, and sticky availability on mobile viewports.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Border of `1.5px` solid `#1B4332`, transparent interior, color transitions to muted green on tap.

### Cards (Agro-tourism & Homestays)
- Background `#FAF9F6`, `1rem` radius, subtle `0.5px` border in `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.
- Image frames occupy the top 55% of the card with an aspect ratio of 4:3 or 16:9, featuring a slight zoom transform on pointer hover.
- Badges (e.g., "Edukasi Kopi", "Homestay Bersertifikat") float at the top right with semi-opaque backgrounds.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-shaped selectors (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
- Inactive state: `#FAF9F6` background with `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.15)` border.
- Active state: `#1B4332` background with `#FAF9F6` typography.

### Form Inputs
- Rounded `0.5rem`, `#FAF9F6` background, inset padding `space-md`.
- Focus border transitions sharply to `#2D6A4F` with a soft 3px ambient ring of `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`.

### Interactive Map & Gallery Modules
- Embedded map containers feature rounded `1rem` outer corners with overlaid quick-navigation badges and custom topographic map markers.
